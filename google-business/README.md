# REFILL · Google Business Profile kit

Everything to paste into Google Business Profile, field by field, in the order Google asks for it.
Items marked **[confirm]** are placeholders: change them to the real facts before you publish.
Google checks the profile against what customers see in person, so true beats clever every time.

## 1. Business name

**Refill Specialty Coffee**

- It matches the logo lockup (REFILL / SPECIALTY COFFEE), so it is the brand's real name, and it
  carries the main search phrase people type.
- Do **not** add the area or extra keywords (for example "Refill Café & Bakery El Shorouk").
  Google treats that as keyword stuffing and can suspend the profile.
- Use exactly the same name on the website, Instagram, signage and delivery apps.

## 2. Categories

| | Category |
|---|---|
| Primary | **Coffee shop** |
| Additional | Cafe · Espresso bar · Bakery · Pastry shop · Breakfast restaurant |
| Add later | Pizza restaurant (only once pizza is actually served) |

Pick each one from Google's dropdown (the wording must match exactly). The primary category matters
most for ranking, so keep it "Coffee shop".

## 4. Opening date

Set **Opening date** to the real opening day **[confirm]**. Google lets you publish a profile before
opening (up to about 90 days ahead): it then shows "Opening soon" on Maps and Search, which starts
collecting followers and searches before day one.

## 5. Location

- Address: **[confirm]** the exact unit, building and street in El Shorouk City, Cairo Governorate.
  Write it exactly as on the lease or utility bill: Google may verify against it.
- Drag the map pin onto the actual entrance, not the middle of the building or the road.
- Storefront: **Yes** (customers visit you). Service area: leave empty.

## 6. Hours

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday to Sunday | 7:00 AM to 12:00 AM **[confirm]** |

- Add **special hours** before Ramadan, Eid and public holidays, so Google never sends people to a
  closed door. Ramadan hours (for example after iftar to suhoor) belong in special hours too.
- When the kitchen and pizza have their own hours, add them under **More hours** (Breakfast,
  Kitchen, Takeout).

## 7. Contact and links

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Phone | **[confirm]** a number that is answered during opening hours (WhatsApp-enabled helps) |
| Website | `https://YOUR-DOMAIN/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp` |
| Menu link | `https://YOUR-DOMAIN/menu` |
| Order link | `https://YOUR-DOMAIN/order` (or the delivery app link, once you have one) |
| Social profiles | Instagram, Facebook, TikTok: the brand handles **[confirm]** |

The `utm_` tags let Google Analytics show how many visits come from the profile.

## 10. Photos

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `png/logo-1080.png` | **Logo** (square, espresso with the plaster cup) |
| `png/cover-1920x1080.png` | **Cover photo** (everything is in the centre, so Google's crops stay safe) |
| `png/post-opening-1200x900.png` | Image for the first update (section 11) |

After opening, add **real photos** every week: the façade by day and night (helps people find the
door), the bar, the pastry case, the room, coffee and pastries on the table, the team at work.
Profiles with fresh photos get noticeably more calls and direction requests.

Do not upload the AI visualisations from the website or campaign as photos of the café. Google
removes images that don't show the real place, and customers notice.

## 11. First update (post)

Use **Add update** with `png/post-opening-1200x900.png` and the button **Learn more** → website:

> Something good is brewing in El Shorouk. Refill opens soon: specialty coffee, a bakehouse that
> laminates its croissants by hand every night, and a calm room made for staying a little longer.
> Pizza follows after dark. Follow along for the opening date. قريباً في الشروق.

Post an update every week or two (new bake, bean of the week, events). Updates keep the profile
active in Google's eyes.

## 12. Replying to reviews

Reply to every review within a day or two, in the language it was written in.

**5 stars, English:** Thank you, [name]. That is exactly the kind of day we hope to refill. See you
for the next cup.

**5 stars, Arabic:** شكراً يا [الاسم]، كلامك ملّى يومنا. مستنيينك في الكوباية الجاية.

**3 stars:** Thank you for the honest note, [name]. We would love to hear what would have made it a
five. Message us on Instagram or ask for the manager next time, and the next coffee is on us.

**1 or 2 stars:** We're sorry, [name]. This isn't the visit we want for anyone. Please contact us at
[phone or email] so we can make it right.

Never offer something in exchange for a review, and never ask only happy customers to review (both
break Google's rules). The rating page on the website (`/rate`) already invites 4 and 5 cup ratings
to Google once `googleReviewUrl` is set in `website/site.config.json`.

## 13. Once the profile is live

- Copy the profile's **"Ask for reviews"** link into `website/site.config.json` → `googleReviewUrl`,
  and the Maps link into `maps`. The website's Directions button and Google review prompt then switch
  on by themselves.
- Keep name, address, phone and hours identical everywhere (website, Instagram, delivery apps).
